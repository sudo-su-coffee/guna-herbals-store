Orders & Logistics

Our apothecary ships within 2-4 business days. Depending on your location, shipping rituals usually take 5-7 days for standard delivery. We take great care in plastic-free packaging to ensure your remedies arrive in their most potent state.
Once your order has left our sanctuary, a tracking link will be sent to your digital scroll (email). You can follow its progress through the winds until it reaches your door.
If a remedy does not resonate with you, we accept returns of unopened items within 14 days of receipt. Please contact our guides through the portal for a return initiation.
